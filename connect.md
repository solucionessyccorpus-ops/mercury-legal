# Connect / Reconnect Mercury Invoice App

Mercury Invoice App connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit OAuth authorization.

Only authorized representatives of Soluciones S&C CORP may connect or reconnect the application to QuickBooks Online.

The app is used for internal accounting, reconciliation, invoice preparation, and invoice creation after internal approval.
