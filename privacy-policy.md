# Privacy Policy

Mercury Invoice App is an internal accounting and invoicing tool used by Soluciones S&C CORP to prepare, reconcile, and manage invoices in QuickBooks Online.

The application may access QuickBooks Online data authorized by the company account owner, including customers, products/services, invoices, company information, and related accounting records.

The information accessed by the app is used only for internal accounting, reconciliation, invoice preparation, and business recordkeeping.

Mercury Invoice App does not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with third parties.

Access credentials and tokens are stored locally by Soluciones S&C CORP and are not publicly shared.

The application is intended for internal business use only and is not a public commercial application.
