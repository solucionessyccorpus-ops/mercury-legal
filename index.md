# Mercury Invoice App

Mercury Invoice App is an internal accounting and invoicing tool used by Soluciones S&C CORP to prepare, reconcile, and manage invoices in QuickBooks Online.

This application is intended only for authorized internal business use by Soluciones S&C CORP.

The app may connect to QuickBooks Online only after authorization from the company account owner.
