# Disconnect Mercury Invoice App

To disconnect Mercury Invoice App from QuickBooks Online, an authorized representative of Soluciones S&C CORP may revoke the app access from the Intuit or QuickBooks Online app settings.

After disconnecting, the app will no longer be authorized to access QuickBooks Online data until a new authorization is completed.
